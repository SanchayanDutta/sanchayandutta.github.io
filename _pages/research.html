Current Research

My research is in quantum coding theory and computational mathematics. I also work on mathematical questions in optimization and machine learning.

At UC Davis, I work with my advisor Greg Kuperberg on the classification of Pauli stabilizer codes through ten qubits up to local Clifford operations and qubit permutations. In a separate solo project, I use Burnside's lemma and module theory to count equivalence classes of classical linear codes and quantum CSS codes. Hall polynomial recurrences provide the fixed-subspace and fixed-flag counts needed for these computations.

With Suvrit Sra, I work on Riemannian bilevel optimization and the mathematical structure of Transformer models. Our projects study connections between memory in Transformers and first-order optimization methods. We also investigate how AI agents acquire useful context through active inference. I visited his group at TU Munich in summer 2024.

My earlier work with Greg concerned anyonic fault tolerance and quantum algebra, particularly Turaev-Viro codes. My broader interests include the computational universality of unitary modular functors arising from quantum groups at different roots of unity.

In summer 2023, I was a visiting graduate researcher in Anurag Anshu's group at Harvard University. I investigated connections between explicit sum-of-squares lower bounds and the NLTS theorem.
